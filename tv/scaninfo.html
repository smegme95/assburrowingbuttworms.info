SCAN Documentation
---
Version 2.1
---
The SCAN feature (Archive only) automatically cycles through Archived streams, playing a random clip from each to provide a "Channel Surfing" experience independent of viewer interaction. The user may adjust the Scan rate (length of each clip) by dragging the slider - to the right for faster, to the left for slower (at the leftmost position Scan is disabled). By default, Scan is on and set to medium rate.
