WORMTUBE GENERATOR Documentation
---
Version 2.1
---

WORMTUBE GENERATOR is a video collage collaboration between me and its User(s) (possibly you). It selects two sources at random from a playlist of Youtube videos (selected by me), plays them at randomly set speeds and composites them over a base layer of video noise (composed by me).

Each time the GENERATOR button is clicked, the sources are reset in a new random order and playback rate.

The User can modify the video output by applying various effects with the Color Controls located at the top of the VIDEO CONTROLS section of the console.

COLOR CONTROLS
Note: the color controls can also be applied to the Archive and Live sources, with the exception of the KEY and BLEND controls.

HUE SHIFT: Change the color of the video.
SATURATION: Change the saturation of the video.
NEGATIVE: Invert the color of the video.
KEY: Key out a color in the top video layer. Choose None, Red, Blue, or Green.
BLEND 1: Change the opacity of the top video layer. Default: 50%.
BLEND 2: Change the blending mode of the video layers.
RESET: Restore all color control settings to default.
